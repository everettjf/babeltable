BabelTable Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

On-device processing

BabelTable recognizes speech and translates text on your device using Apple's Speech and Translation frameworks. Conversation content is not sent to a cloud translation provider. No account, key, tracking, or advertising SDK is used. Audio is processed in memory and is not saved.

Language model downloads

Prepare speech and translation models while online before first use. Once installed, supported languages work offline. Apple manages model downloads and may collect framework usage and performance metrics; Translation metrics exclude original and translated content. Apple's services are governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Local history and sharing

Conversation drafts, transcripts, translations, duration totals, and limited diagnostics are stored locally. Device backups may include these files. Delete conversations in History and share them only when you choose. Deleting a conversation does not delete exported copies or backups. Capture pauses in the background and during interruptions.

Upgrades

The offline version deletes obsolete saved credentials without reading them. Existing conversations remain readable. Previously shared or remotely processed data is not removed by upgrading.

Full policy and contact

See the full privacy policy or contact us. Do not post private conversations in public issues.
